Meet Isabel

One loving face.
Every single day.

Isabel is the heart and the only caregiver here. That means no rotating staff and no getting lost in a crowded room — just a steady, trusted relationship that helps children feel safe enough to grow.

She brings Spanish naturally into greetings, stories, songs, play and mealtimes. Children begin hearing and using the language from their first day, without pressure or formal drills.
